# IBD Research Digest

A curated literature review on inflammatory bowel disease (Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis), compiled 10 October 2026.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`IBD_Research_Digest.md`](IBD_Research_Digest.md) | Main document: key takeaways, drug-landscape and Mediterranean-diet evidence tables, then 101 entries grouped into 6 parts, each with citation, DOI/PubMed/PMC links, a focus note and the PubMed abstract. Part 7 synthesises how the Mediterranean diet fits alongside drug therapy. |
| [`IBD_Articles_Index.csv`](IBD_Articles_Index.csv) | One row per article (entry number, title, journal, year, type, links, focus note). Opens in Excel or Google Sheets for sorting and filtering. |

**Parts of the digest**
1. Understanding IBD: overviews, epidemiology, pathogenesis, prediction
2. Treatment strategy and current guidelines (STRIDE-II, PROFILE, AGA, ECCO)
3. Current advanced therapies: pivotal and head-to-head trials (IL-23 p19, JAK, S1P, anti-TNF, combination)
4. Developing treatments: anti-TL1A, obefazimod, oral peptides, FMT, cell therapy, anti-fibrotics, AI
5. Diet in IBD: guidelines (ECCO 2025, AGA 2024, ESPEN, IOIBD), EEN/CDED, ultra-processed food
6. Mediterranean diet and IBD: prevention, intervention trials, mechanisms, systematic reviews
7. Synthesis: the Mediterranean diet alongside drug therapy, plus research gaps

*Educational summary only, not medical advice. Abstracts are reproduced from PubMed with attribution.*
